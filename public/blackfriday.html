Upgrade FAQs

Short answers to the questions we hear most.

Will my old PC take an SSD?
In almost all cases, yes. Solid State Drives (SSDs) are compatible with the vast majority of computers made in the last 15 years. If your PC is exceptionally old, we will advise you during the quote process.
Is my data safe during the upgrade?
Absolutely. Our standard HDD-to-SSD upgrade includes full data migration. We clone your existing drive to the new one, ensuring all your files, photos, and applications are exactly where you left them.
What does the “From R999” price include?
The starting price of R999 (VAT included) covers a standard 240GB/256GB SSD, the data transfer service, and the labour involved. If you need a larger drive or a RAM upgrade as well, the final quote will be adjusted accordingly.
How long does an upgrade take?
Most PC and laptop upgrades are completed within 24 to 48 hours. During the Black Friday rush it can take a little longer, so we will give you a clear timeframe when you drop off your device.
